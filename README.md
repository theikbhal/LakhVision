# LakhVision

**₹20,00,000 in 2 Years — Visual Goal App** (macOS, Swift/SwiftUI)

Built for a Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh household: reach ₹20 lakhs in 2 years after 4 years with no job and no self-earning income. Goal set by wife.

## Features

1. **Many goal pass-mark ways** — ring, thermometer, money bar, days split, 100-block grid, gullak fill, cash bricks, pass-mark ladder (25/50/75/100%), pace vs time; ₹20L target highlighted
2. **Currency notes** — ₹500 / ₹200 / ₹100 stacks, optimal mix, bundles (100×₹500 = ₹50k), cash weight
3. **Anything** — car, house
4. **Gold** — grams + **sovereigns** (8g), rate editable
5. **Farmland** — acres + **guntas** near Tirupati (1 acre = 40 guntas)
6. **Tractor, Auto, Car** — affordability cards
7. **Why card** — wife set the goal · no job 4 years · no self income
8. **Menu bar** — saved · % · streak · quick-add
9. **Push notifications** — daily visit, weekly minimum, monthly worst-case + milestone alerts
10. **Installed to** `/Applications/LakhVision.app`
11. **GitHub repo** — https://github.com/theikbhal/LakhVision
12. **Mac app (Swift)**
13. **Check cadence** — daily visit · weekly minimum · worst case once a month (with **streak**)
14. **Registered in AppTracker** (Applications tracker)

### Extra

- **History tab** — trajectory vs ideal pace (Swift Charts), monthly level bars, 28-day visit grid
- **Forecast** — projected finish date at current pace
- **Streaks** — current/best daily visit streak
- **Dock badge** — shows goal %
- **App icon**
